# QtyRight — Market Research, Niche Selection & Business Strategy

*Prepared 2026-10-31. Every figure below is either (a) taken from a named source listed in
§10, or (b) explicitly labelled an estimate/assumption. No search volume, CPC, RPM or
revenue figure in this document was invented: where a tool's exact US volume could not be
verified for free, this report says so and scores the niche on SERP evidence instead.*

---

## 1. Executive summary

15 online-tools niches were researched and scored out of 100 on the user's weighted model.
The winner is **US home-improvement & construction material calculators** — "how much do I
need to buy?" tools (concrete, paint, mulch, gravel, tile, flooring, drywall, fence, deck,
brick, topsoil, lumber). Final score **86/100**, ahead of US paycheck calculators (79) and
solar/home-energy calculators (77).

The brand built for it is **QtyRight** — *"Get the quantity right before you buy."*
The differentiator against a fast-crowding field of 2026 calculator clones is not the math
(the math is a commodity) but the **buying decision**: every QtyRight calculator returns a
printable shopping list, an editable-price cost estimate, and — where it applies — a
**bags-vs-bulk / bags-vs-ready-mix break-even comparison**, which is the actual question a
US homeowner standing in a Home Depot aisle is trying to answer.

## 2. What the evidence actually shows (verified findings)

1. **The calculator model works at scale, monetised by display ads.** Omni Calculator
   (3,700+ calculators) received 15.18M visits in September 2026 (Semrush, via search
   results); its business model is reported as primarily Google AdSense advertising.
   Inch Calculator — focused primarily on construction/home improvement — received
   **5.21M visits in September 2026, 58.5% of them from the United States (3.05M)**,
   with **71.68% of traffic from Google organic**. That is direct proof that a
   home-calculator site can attract large, mostly-US, mostly-organic traffic.
2. **Home-improvement advertisers pay well.** WordStream/LocaliQ benchmarks put US
   search CPC for *Home & Home Improvement* at **$7.85 (2025)** rising to **$8.33
   (2026)** — among the highest of all industries (all-industry median $5.42 in 2026).
   Display RPM for the niche is estimated by third parties in a wide band — **$5–7 RPM**
   (OrganicArbitrage's AdSense-by-niche analysis) up to **$20–40 RPM** (Adstimate's
   2026 publisher benchmark). *These are third-party estimates, not guarantees; this
   plan assumes the conservative end.*
3. **The underlying US market is huge and durable.** US home-improvement expenditure
   was roughly **$531B in 2025** (Statista topic page); other industry round-ups put
   2025 renovation/improvement spending near **$509B** and total market size near
   **$594B**. The median US home is ~42 years old, which sustains repair demand.
4. **Some "obvious" niches are traps.** PDF tools: iLovePDF alone had **325.13M visits**
   in September 2026 (Semrush) with a 92 authority score — a new site cannot realistically
   displace it. Paycheck calculators: SmartAsset (4.77M visits, **93.8% US**) ranks #3
   for "paycheck calculator" (Semrush-listed US volume 550,000/mo, CPC $4.52) and ADP
   also occupies the SERP — plus the content must be re-verified against federal/state
   tax law every year. Solar: the **30% US federal residential solar credit expired
   31 Dec 2025**, so solar ROI content built in 2024–25 is now materially wrong across
   most competitor sites — a policy-volatility warning for that niche.
5. **The home-calculator field is crowding fast — this is the main risk.** Live SERP
   checks on 2026-10-31 surfaced many very new entrants (ProjectHomeCalc, DwellQuant,
   YardaCalc, BagsAndYards, MeasureHaven, BlueprintCalc, HowManyCalc and more), several
   crawled within hours. Two conclusions follow: (a) Google *is* ranking new,
   low-authority home-calculator sites for long-tail queries, so entry is possible;
   (b) a me-too clone will not win — differentiation (§6) and guide content are required.

## 5. Top 3 deep-dive

### A. Home-improvement material calculators — 86
- **Audience:** US homeowners (DIY), plus small contractors/handymen. 86.9M owner-occupied
  US homes; improvement spending ~$509–594B/yr.
- **Why they'd use it:** a wrong material order costs a second store trip mid-project or
  hundreds of dollars of un-returnable bulk material. The search happens *at the moment
  of purchase intent* — the most valuable moment for advertisers.
- **Competitor weaknesses:** Inch Calculator is broad and ad-heavy; Omni is generic and
  metric-first; retailer calculators (Home Depot/Lowe's/Quikrete) only compute their own
  SKUs and push product; the 2026 clone wave computes a volume and stops — no cost,
  no bags-vs-bulk decision, no printable list.
- **Feasibility:** pure browser maths (volume/area formulas, published bag yields) —
  no API, no server, no accounts, no yearly law changes. Formulas are stable for decades.

### B. US paycheck calculators — 79
- Verified demand is the strongest of any niche ("paycheck calculator" 550k/mo, CPC
  $4.52; "tax calculator" 1.83M/mo — Semrush figures surfaced via SmartAsset's profile).
- **Why it loses:** the SERP is held by ADP, SmartAsset (93.8% US traffic) and
  Calculator.net; correct output needs current federal + 50-state + locality tax logic
  re-verified every tax year; a wrong net-pay number is a trust-ending error; and the
  natural monetisation is advisor lead-gen, not AdSense.

### C. Solar & home-energy calculators — 77
- Highest advertiser intent per visitor (installer leads are worth a great deal).
- **Why it loses *now*:** the expiry of the 30% federal residential credit on
  31 Dec 2025 invalidated the core ROI maths most content is built on; credible output
  needs sun-hours, utility-rate and incentive data by state (maintenance-heavy); the
  incumbents (EnergySage, SolarReviews) monetise via lead sales and outspend AdSense
  sites for the same queries. A good *second* site once QtyRight has authority.

### Final selection: Home-improvement material calculators (QtyRight)
Wins because it is the only top-3 niche that is simultaneously (i) proven to draw
millions of mostly-US organic visits, (ii) backed by top-tier advertiser CPCs,
(iii) buildable 100% free in the browser with stable, verifiable maths, and
(iv) still showing new low-authority sites ranking for long-tail queries today.

**Risks that could prevent success (stated plainly):**
1. **Crowding:** dozens of similar sites launched in 2026. Mitigation: buying-decision
   features (cost + break-even + printable list), guide content, and long-tail program
  matic-but-hand-written pages (common sizes, e.g. "concrete for a 10×10 slab").
2. **Seasonality:** outdoor-project searches peak in spring/early summer and dip in
   winter. Mitigation: indoor tools (paint, drywall, flooring, tile) carry winter.
3. **Incumbent authority:** Inch Calculator/Calculator.net will keep most head terms
   for a long time. The plan targets long-tails first; head terms are a year-2 goal.
4. **AdSense RPM uncertainty:** third-party niche RPM estimates range $5–40. The
   business must work at the low end; anything above is upside.
5. **Zero-click/AI answers:** Google may answer simple formulas directly. Mitigation:
   multi-input tools (openings, waste, price, bag size) can't be answered in a snippet.

## 6. Business strategy (Phase 3)

- **Brand:** QtyRight — *"Get the quantity right before you buy."* Visual identity:
  deep spruce green + warm amber on off-white, tape-measure motif, no gradients,
  no generic stock-photo hero. Trust signals: formula shown on every page, waste
  factor explained, "estimates, not a contractor quote" disclaimer.
- **Domain ideas (NOT purchased; availability not verified — check on a registrar
  before relying on any):** qtyright.com, qtyrightcalc.com, getqtyright.com,
  qtyrighttools.com. The code uses `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` so the domain can be
  attached later without code changes.
- **Audiences:** Primary US; secondary UK/CA/AU (imperial units are shared with CA
  in practice; metric toggle is a Phase-2 expansion item).
- **Launch tools (12):** Concrete, Paint, Mulch, Gravel, Tile, Flooring, Drywall,
  Fence, Deck, Brick & Block, Topsoil, Board Foot.
- **Every tool returns:** primary quantity, whole units to actually buy (bags/sheets/
  boxes rounded up), estimated cost from an editable unit price, a printable
  shopping list, the formula, a worked example, FAQs, related tools.
- **Signature feature:** bags-vs-bulk (mulch, topsoil, gravel) and bags-vs-ready-mix
  (concrete) cost comparison — the decision competitors skip.
- **Future expansion:** Paver, Sod, Sand, Roofing Shingles, Wallpaper, Insulation,
  Cubic Yard (generic), Stair Stringer, Concrete Steps, Asphalt, Fence Post Concrete,
  plus "common size" landing pages and a metric toggle.
- **Structure:** `/` home · `/[tool]-calculator/` tools · `/guides/` content ·
  `/about /contact /privacy-policy /terms /disclaimer /faq`.
- **SEO content strategy:** one page per tool (no duplicates), 3 launch guides,
  internal linking tool↔guide↔related tools, FAQ + WebApplication/HowTo-eligible
  schema only where accurate, sitemap + canonicals + OG from day one.
- **AdSense strategy:** configurable `AdSlot` components (in-content after results,
  sidebar-on-desktop equivalent block, guide mid-article). Nothing renders until a
  publisher ID is set via env. No ads near buttons/inputs (accidental-click policy),
  no fake ad units.

## 7.1 Owner rule (added 2026-10-31): keyword difficulty must be under 20

The site owner set a hard filter after the initial research: **only target
keywords with a difficulty score under 20**, so pages can realistically rank
without a large backlink budget.

Honest limitation: no paid Ahrefs/Semrush/Mangools account is available in this
project, so exact third-party KD numbers cannot be verified for free, and this
document does not invent them. Instead, targets were screened with the standard
free proxy for KD < 20 — **the live SERP itself**: a keyword is treated as
"KD < 20 in practice" when its current top 10 contains new/low-authority sites,
forums, or thin pages (exactly the pattern the 2026-10-31 SERP checks found for
home-calculator long-tails). After launch, every target below must be confirmed
with a real KD tool (free trials are enough) and with Search Console data, and
any keyword that measures ≥ 20 is dropped to a secondary/supporting role.

## 8. 30-day growth plan (post-launch)

- **Days 1–3:** Deploy (Vercel), attach domain if bought, verify in Search Console,
  submit sitemap, set up GA4 (optional env var), check all pages indexable.
- **Week 1:** Publish 2 more guides (concrete slab sizes table; paint coverage by
  room size). Add "common sizes" quick-answer blocks to concrete/paint/mulch pages.
- **Week 2:** Add Paver + Sod calculators (top requested outdoor tools). Internal-link
  every guide to its tool and vice-versa.
- **Week 3:** Outreach-lite: answer 10 genuine Reddit r/DIY / r/HomeImprovement and
  forum questions with worked maths linking the relevant calculator (no spam —
  full answer in the post). Submit to tool directories.
- **Week 4:** Review Search Console queries; expand pages for any query with
  impressions but position > 20; fix any Core Web Vitals flags; apply for AdSense
  once the site has indexed pages, complete policy pages and steady early traffic
  (approval is Google's decision — not guaranteed).
